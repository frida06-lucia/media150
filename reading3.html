Reading 3


Graphic Novel: Motherhood In Crisis


Reading through these women’s stories in the form of a graphic novel was much more engaging snd interesting that reading it as a typical short story. The stories that these young women have to share are heartbreaking but sadly not surprising as there is violence and assault against women everywhere. The stories being told through this medium of graphic novel further helped me visualize these women and not just leave them as words on a screen. However though this medium there is also a sense of disconnect as you see an animated/comic version of a person and think “Well this person isn’t real”. But contrasting this medium with real pictures and videos of the women doing the outreach in these communities demonstrates that these are not made up stories.

The choice to have these stories told through this medium could also have been impacted by the want and need to get these stories more accessible to the public and spread awareness to the lack of proper health care. The testimonies of the doctors and outreach staff really help illustrate this picture. A theme in these stories is the refusal for more traditional medical treatments like herbs and such, because these women have been put through so much trauma and turmoil because of these practices and there advice it is often the medical professionals that have to step in.

Through there work in these organizations and helping out certian communities you can tell how much these workers and people care for and want to help the women they encounter. There testimonies at the end of each graphic novel helps remind us that these are real people with real stories. This inclusion of the womens stories also does not make it feel as if they are fishing for donations or begging for sympathy, they are showcasing the stories of these women, who have allowed there stories to be told. I also appricated the decison to leave these women essentially not known, if they dont want to be. Keeping these women as animated and comic helps perserve there identity if they would like to be. In another vain the women who have decided to share there photos make much more of an impact because you see these women and that they are real and not just characters on a screen that I feel just a short story would have protrayed.
