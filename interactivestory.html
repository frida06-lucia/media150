The Day of the Dead: One Bite






You jolt awake for late nights rest, your sweating bullets and shaking. The Group around you is fast asleep. The air feels different, everything is the same in the woods you have slept in for months but its different somehow. You notice a pain on your arm, a small bite on arm, tiny, but still there. You know you will become fully infected in a few days maybe by morning. You sit in shock, what do you do?

  • Alert the Group

  • Leave Group

  • Go back to sleep
